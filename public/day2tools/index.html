Slide 2 · Operational Protocol

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

SOP-ADM-004: Sequential Value Modification & Posture-Based Routing

1. Purpose & Scope

To standardize the transmission and conditional modification of incoming integer values across three verification desks without calculation error or unrecorded omissions.

2. Responsible Roles

  • Officer 1: Initial Processing & Base Computation.
  • Officer 2: Pocket State Verification.
  • Officer 3: Chest Fold Posture Verification.

3. Prerequisites & Inputs

A verified integer supplied by the Facilitator (Director/PS equivalent) and clear line-of-sight to the Facilitator's physical posture.

4. Numbered Procedure Steps

  1. Step 1 (Desk 1): Receive base figure $N$. Immediately calculate $N_1 = N + 5$. Verbally or physically transfer $N_1$ to Desk 2.
  2. Step 2 (Desk 2): Receive $N_1$. Visually observe the Facilitator.
    • If hands are inside pockets: calculate $N_2 = N_1 + 2$.
    • If hands are outside pockets: $N_2 = N_1$ (no modification).
    Transfer $N_2$ to Desk 3.
  3. Step 3 (Desk 3): Receive $N_2$. Visually observe the Facilitator.
    • If arms/hands are folded across chest: calculate $N_3 = N_2 + 1$.
    • If arms/hands are not folded: $N_3 = N_2$ (no modification).
  4. Step 4: Announce $N_3$ as the validated final output.

5. Exceptions & Controls

Obscured View: If an officer's view of the Facilitator is blocked, the officer must pause the file and request visual confirmation rather than guessing.

💡 Tab 3: Tacit Wisdom

  • The Common Trap: "New officers focus entirely on mental math and forget to look up at the Facilitator."
  • Transitional Postures: If the Facilitator is withdrawing their hands mid-calculation, evaluate the stance at the exact second the number crossed your desk.
  • Escalation: In case of discrepancy, summon the Observer note immediately.
