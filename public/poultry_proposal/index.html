Operational Lifecycle Roadmap (85–90 Weeks)

Step-by-step biological and commercial milestones from facility commissioning to spent layer salvage

Weeks 0 – 2

Facility Lease & Battery Cage Setup

Discharge ₦1.5M farm facility rent. Install ₦1.0M galvanized 3-tier battery cages with automatic nipple drinker pipelines and anti-waste feed troughs. Disinfect pen and commission brooder heating.

Weeks 1 – 8

Brooding & Chick Starter Phase

Stock 500 commercial layer DOCs (@ ₦3,700). Maintain regulated brooding heat, anti-stress protocols, and rigorous Lasota/Gumboro immunization courses. Consume ~30 bags starter feed.

Weeks 9 – 18

Grower Phase & Cage Transfer

Transition to grower mash (~94 bags). Weekly sample weighing to maintain strict target bodyweight. Cautious beak trimming and transfer into battery cages at Week 16 to acclimate birds before lay onset.

Weeks 19 – 24

Point-of-Lay (POL) & Ramp-Up

First pullet eggs emerge at Week 18–20. Lay rate climbs rapidly from 30% to 75%+. Feed transitions to high-calcium Layer Mash. Working capital reserve covers operational costs during this ramp-up.

Weeks 25 – 75

Peak Production & Sustained Monthly Cash Flow

Flock stabilizes at 88–92% Hen-Day production (~420–450 eggs/day, producing ~440–460 crates per month). Generates consistent net monthly operating surplus of ~₦785k+, distributed 60% Investor / 40% Operator.

Weeks 76 – 90

Flock Liquidation & Terminal Salvage

Production tapers below the economic threshold (<65%). Surviving ~430 spent layers are sold into the live meat market at ₦6,000/bird (yielding ~₦2,580,000), split 60/40. Battery cages are cleaned and prepped for Batch 2.

Key Operating Safeguards & Loss Prevention

Strict Operating Protocols
Feed Efficiency & Zero Wastage

Feed is >75% of operational expense. Galvanized cages with roll-lipped feed troughs prevent feed scooping and eliminate feed waste by 8–12%.

Biosecurity & Zero Coccidiosis

Battery cages isolate birds completely from litter and droppings, virtually eliminating coccidiosis and internal parasites, protecting 95% flock livability.

Egg Integrity & Guaranteed Off-Take

Sloped wire floors roll fresh eggs immediately away from birds, preventing pecking and cracks. Bulk supply arrangements with bakeries ensure steady off-take.
